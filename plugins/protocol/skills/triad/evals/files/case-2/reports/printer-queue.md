# Printer queue problems

The label printer queue stopped four times in two weeks. Each time a job
of more than 200 labels was sent from the shipping PC. The queue shows
"error" and holds every later job until someone restarts the spooler.
The driver on the shipping PC is two versions older than on the other
PCs. A test with 500 labels from the front desk PC, which has the newer
driver, went through. Proposal: update the driver on the shipping PC,
then repeat the 500 label test from there.
